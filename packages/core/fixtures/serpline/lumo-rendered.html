Product details

Pro X is our quietest pair yet. Six microphones listen to the room around you and cancel steady noise such as engines, air conditioning and traffic, while voices stay clear when you switch to transparency mode with one tap on the left ear cup.

Noise cancelling

Adaptive noise cancelling checks the sound around you many times per second and adjusts on its own, so a busy train and a quiet office both feel calm. Wind noise reduction keeps calls clear when you walk outside, and a dedicated mode for flights lowers the deep hum of the engines.

Battery and charging

One charge lasts up to 30 hours with noise cancelling on and up to 40 hours with it off. Ten minutes on the cable gives about five hours of music, and the headphones go to sleep when you take them off, so the battery is ready when you need it again.
