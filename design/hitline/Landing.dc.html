FAQ

Questions

Something else? Ask me directly

Is Hitline really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no account and no usage limit.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. Everything is analyzed in your browser. Only your settings are saved, and only on your computer.

How does it detect server-side GTM?

It checks where GA4 requests go, the request path, the server_container_url setting and cookies set by the server.

How is it different from Tag Assistant?

Tag Assistant focuses on Google tags. Hitline shows Google, Meta and TikTok side by side, checks consent and attribution, and compares events with your tracking plan.

Do I need DevTools open?

The popup works without it. For the live event stream, open DevTools before the page loads, or reload once.
