FAQ

Questions

Something else? Ask me directly

Is Rivaline really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no account and no usage limit.

Does it send any data anywhere?

No. Rivaline reads the page in your browser and keeps the result in memory until you close the tab. Only your settings are saved, and only on your computer.

How does it recognize tools?

It matches script addresses, cookie names, page globals and meta tags against its own list of marketing tools. Every match shows the evidence it found.

Can it show a competitor's ad spend?

No. Ad libraries show which ads are running, not budgets. Rivaline opens those libraries with the search already filled in.

Why do I click the icon again on a new tab?

Rivaline uses Chrome's activeTab permission. It can read a page only after you ask, so it never has access to every site you visit.
