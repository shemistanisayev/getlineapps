FAQ

Questions

Something else? Ask me directly

Is Markline really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no account, no watermark you have to pay to remove and no limit on captures.

Where do my screenshots go?

Nowhere. A capture opens in an editor tab on your computer. You share it by downloading a file or copying it to the clipboard. Close the tab and it is gone.

How does full page capture work?

Markline scrolls the page one screen at a time, takes a screenshot of each and joins them. Fixed and sticky parts are hidden after the first screen so they don't repeat. Very long pages are cut at the size Chrome can draw, and Markline tells you when that happens.

Is a blurred area really hidden?

Yes. The blur is painted into the exported image, and the original pixels under it are not in the file. For the strongest result, pick the solid style.

Can I remove the "Made with Markline" line?

Yes. Turn it off in the export window or in Settings. It is a small line of text, and it stays off once you switch it off.

Why do I click the icon again on a new tab?

Markline uses Chrome's activeTab permission. It can capture a page only after you ask, so it never has access to every site you visit.
