FAQ

Questions

Something else? Ask me directly

Is Serpline really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no account and no usage limit.

How is it different from Ahrefs or Screaming Frog?

Those crawl whole sites from a server or from your desktop. Serpline checks the page you are on, inside your browser, including what changes after JavaScript and what AI crawlers are allowed to read. It works well next to them.

How does it check AI search?

It reads robots.txt and llms.txt on the site, matches the rules for each known AI crawler and compares the raw HTML with the rendered page, because most AI crawlers don't run JavaScript.

Does it crawl my whole site?

No. It checks the open page, the site's robots.txt and llms.txt, and the status of internal links on that page.

Does it send my pages anywhere?

No. Requests go only to the site you are on, from your own browser. Nothing reaches me or anyone else.
