FAQ

Questions people ask

Not answered here? Ask me directly.

Are the tools really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan and no trial. They are free because they show the kind of work I do for clients: if you need the fixes done, you know where to find me.

What data do the extensions collect?

None. Each extension saves only its own settings on your computer. The pages you check are read in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.

Do they slow down Chrome?

No. Nothing runs in the background. A tool only works on a tab after you click its icon or press its shortcut there.

Which one should I install first?

Pick your role in the tool list above. If you run paid campaigns, start with Hitline: broken tracking costs the most and is the hardest to see.

Can I use them for client work?

Yes. Agencies use the PDF reports in audits and pitches. The reports carry a small "Made with" line and nothing else.

Which languages do they support?

English and Azerbaijani. You can switch the language in each tool's settings.
